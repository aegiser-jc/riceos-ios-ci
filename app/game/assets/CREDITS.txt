Nalamun artwork and original Canvas characters: RiceOS field art pass, 2026.
Reference palette: Thai paddy field visit, 7 November 2026.
Rice plant cut-outs and mud, water, grass and road tiles generated with
FLUX.2-klein-4B (Apache-2.0), then selected, recoloured and reduced locally.
Dry cracked mud and loose straw texture generation:
Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (Apache-2.0), recoloured and reduced locally.
Both model licences: Apache License, Version 2.0.
Source candidates and model provenance retained in the development art records.
Prompt SemiBold and Sarabun Regular: SIL Open Font License 1.1;
licence texts accompany the fonts in assets/fonts/.
Original sound cues: synthesized locally for this game.

Pass 5 backdrops (tall and wide), buffalo views, egret, sala, sugar palm,
banana, bush, hat post, tied sheaves and original seed friend in three poses:
FLUX.2-klein-4B (Apache-2.0), generated for Nalamun with the same painted
style reference as the rice. Backdrops use the source WebP colours to retain
smooth cloud and reflection shading. Sprites use the pass 5 palette; the
friend body uses jade greens from that palette. Local lossless WebP packing.
Additional pass 5 painted bank, two heading clumps, three ripe drooping
clumps and three neighbour farmers: FLUX.2-klein-4B (Apache-2.0), created
with the same style reference, keyed and locked to the pass 5 palette.
Heading foliage recoloured to the field greens; one broad-leaf plant rejected.
